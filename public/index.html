How this was built

After every coffee chat at PSE UofM, the new member sends the brother a thank-you email and copies the Professional Development inbox. This map rebuilds all 2,301 of those emails from Chi, Psi and Omega: each dot is a person and each line is a chat.

Only names, pledge classes, years and chat dates are shown. No email addresses or email contents are published.

  • Workshops, panels, recruiter emails, apologies and duplicates are removed.
  • People are matched by email address across nicknames.
  • Chats with the 45 seniors who have since graduated are kept.
  • Exchange students are left out. Omega runs through November 7, 2026.
  • The months between pledge classes are skipped on the timeline.

A chat without a thank-you email doesn't appear here, so a quiet dot can mean missing paperwork rather than a missing chat.
